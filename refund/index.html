Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains refunds and cancellations for downloadable digital products sold by XENARO.

Digital Product Purchases

Because XENARO products are digital files delivered electronically, completed purchases are generally non-refundable once the product has been successfully delivered or made available for download, except where required by applicable law.

When a Refund May Be Considered

  • You were charged but the product was not made available because of a technical issue on our side.
  • The downloaded file is materially defective and we are unable to provide a working replacement.
  • A duplicate payment was successfully charged for the same order.
  • A refund is otherwise required by applicable law or the payment provider's rules.

When a Refund Is Normally Not Available

  • You changed your mind after the digital product was delivered.
  • You purchased without checking listed software, version, or plugin requirements.
  • You no longer need or want the file after receiving access.

Cancellations

If payment has not been completed, you may cancel or close the payment process. Once a successful payment has been verified and the digital product delivered, the order cannot normally be cancelled.

Requesting Support

If you believe there is a genuine issue with your purchase, contact us through the Contact page with your registered email address and relevant payment/order details.
